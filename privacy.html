GPU Guard

Privacy policy

GPU Guard is private, owner-operated monitoring and automation software. It is not offered as a public hosted service.

Google user data

Google account access is granted by the account holder through Google's OAuth consent flow. The permissions granted and the operator's instructions determine which data the software can access. This can include Gmail messages and metadata; calendar events; Drive files; Docs and Sheets content; and contact information. Authorized tasks can read, create, modify or delete permitted content. Monitoring notifications use Gmail to send operational messages to a recipient designated by the operator.

Use and processing

Data is used to perform operator-requested tasks and maintain the automation. Depending on the task, data may be processed by owner-operated tools and by automation, AI or service providers selected by the operator. Access should be limited to what the task requires. This application does not offer advertising, sell Google user data or use Google user data to develop or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

Credentials are managed separately from this public website in the operator's credential storage. Task records, alert queues and diagnostic logs may be retained by the operator for operation, troubleshooting and recovery. Retention and deletion of those records are controlled by the operator; data held by Google remains subject to the account's settings and Google's policies.

This website

This static information website does not request Google permissions, collect account credentials or publish account contents or monitoring data. It uses no application analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary connection information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Control and contact

You can revoke the application's Google access in Google Account connections. For access, privacy, retained-data or deletion questions, contact pisces.assistant@gmail.com. Revocation prevents future authorized access but does not itself delete records already retained by the operator.

GPU Guard's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
